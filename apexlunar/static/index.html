Brightness
How bright. The lunar output's level for a full moon well above the horizon. Other nights scale down by how much of the moon is lit: a half moon gets half of this, a thin crescent a few percent.
How fast it brightens. At moonrise the light starts at 0 and climbs until the moon is this many degrees above the horizon, then holds. Low = quick fade-in; high (60–90°) = keeps brightening until the moon is highest.
More rows follow the moon's curve more closely. Rows that wouldn't change anything are skipped.
Starting point: 15% and 30°. Watch the tank on a full-moon night and adjust.
Apex
Pick a Radion XR15's lunar output (Apex type 15G6PL, on the MXM module). With several Radions, group their lunar outputs in Apex Fusion and pick the group master. Other MXM lights may work but are untested.

Daily update checking…

A small background job, run by Windows Task Scheduler, writes each new day's table, so this page doesn't need to stay open. It also runs a minute after you log in, to catch up if the computer was off or asleep.
